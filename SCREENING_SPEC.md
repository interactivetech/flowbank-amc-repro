# Corrected DeepMath screening specification v1

Dataset: `zwhe99/DeepMath-103K` at `5cf055d1fe3d7a2eb19719ac020211469736ae44`. Source row identity is the zero-based concatenation of the ten lexically ordered train parquet shards. Each shard hash is recorded. The previous 26 row IDs and normalized question texts are excluded after verifying their source mapping. Remaining questions are deduplicated by Unicode NFKC and whitespace normalization.

Model: `RedHatAI/Qwen3.8-27B-INT4` at `e45c27e8f463817db788d141a73a3c235a0a7c0c`. One B300, tensor parallelism one, concurrency two, thinking enabled, no speculative decoding, context 262,144. The existing deployment resolves INT4 weights and FP8 KV cache. Setup and `/models` checks are not inference.

Sampled requests: temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, explicit top-k 20, presence/frequency penalties zero, repetition penalty one, and 131,072 generated tokens. The only user input is the existing GENERAL prompt populated with the question, without reference, difficulty, or screening metadata. Seed: `(42 * 1_000_003 + dataset_row * 32 + rollout_index) & 0x7fffffff`, rollout indices 0–31. Retry the same slot with the same seed. Four transport attempts per visit; exhausted failures leave slots incomplete. Full request and response evidence is durable on scratch before grading.

One separate greedy control is recorded for every fully screened question (before its last sampled pair, to preserve the control at a budget boundary): temperature zero, top-p one, top-k disabled, seed 42. Greedy results never determine promotion, selection, or bucket membership. Two known-answer synthetic calibration controls precede candidates and cannot be selected. Calibration and greedy inference count against the same budget.

Freeze a Random(42) shuffle within integer source-difficulty bands, then interleave bands round-robin from highest to lowest. Grade references before candidate inference and quarantine unsupported references. Triage uses eight sampled responses. Complete 32 first for candidates whose projected count (`round(correct * 32 / completed)`) would fill a missing bucket. Retain deferred candidates; after the first traversal, complete them in frozen order if time remains. Selection ties use the original candidate order. Selection uses no workflow outcomes or greedy responses.

Final buckets require 32 completed sampled slots: 0, 1–8, 9–16, 17–24, 25–32 correct. Target five per bucket. A completed response with an unextractable final answer is a parse failure and an observed unsuccessful response; a grader exception quarantines the candidate, while an infrastructure failure is never counted as a mathematical error. Truncated responses retain their finish reason and are graded from their saved final content.

At most 86,400 active seconds across allocations: wall time while the screening engine runs, with concurrency counted once. Preparation, environment setup, queueing, deployment startup, and allocation downtime are excluded. The ledger checkpoints every second and on graceful interruption. Each heartbeat also reserves a conservative 60-second interruption allowance. Graceful shutdown releases it; abrupt recovery charges that tail, never allocation downtime. Status includes the outstanding reserve as an upper bound. Allocation signals cancel outstanding HTTP calls, preserving completed responses and unfinished slots. Screening stops at the remaining active-time deadline. Source/environment/provenance changes fail resume and require a new committed version or an explicit consistent grading correction.

Export provisional selections, full sampled diagnostics, a budget ledger, and a private review packet. Each selected reference needs a named human reviewer. All incorrect sampled responses in the two lowest-rate buckets must be reviewed individually. Audit acceptance binds to a digest of all 32 saved graded responses; changed evidence invalidates acceptance. Quarantine unresolved cases and take the next eligible candidate in frozen order. No locked manifest is emitted until every selected item passes. At budget exhaustion, a smaller audited set may be locked, with shortfalls retained; no substitute labels or buckets are invented.
